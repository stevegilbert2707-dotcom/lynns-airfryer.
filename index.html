Food safety and nutrition

Built-in nutrition values and recipe totals are approximate. They are not a substitute for an authoritative matching food record or product label. Imported values are not independently verified by this app.

Use manufacturer and packaging instructions first. Check the centre of food with a clean food thermometer when appropriate. UK guidance includes 70°C for 2 minutes or equivalent combinations. Take extra care with poultry, pork and minced-meat products.
